Turn a suspicious email into an evidence report.

Say it “nerd-trace.” The name plays on Baynard.

NardTrace is an email and infrastructure investigation workbench. Investigate an email, IP address, domain, URL or file hash, and get a report you can send to a provider, a platform, or law enforcement. Every finding says how it is known and what it does not prove.

The web edition runs entirely in your browser: your email never leaves your device. Only read-only DNS and registry lookups go out.

Six modules, one case file

Analyses attach to a case. The case keeps the original evidence, a chain-of-custody log, and your notes, and exports everything as one report.

Email Analyzer

Full Received-hop route, the hand-off server into your provider, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, From/Reply-To mismatches, display-name spoofing, lookalike domains, links and attachments.

IP Intelligence

Registry owner and ASN via RDAP, the network's abuse contact, reverse DNS, approximate network-level location, optional reputation.

Domain Intelligence

DNS records, SPF and DMARC policy, registrar and abuse contact, domain-age warnings, lookalike detection.

URL Analyzer

Defangs links and decodes Safe Links, Proofpoint and redirect wrappers from the URL text. It never visits the link.

File & Hash

MD5, SHA-1 and SHA-256, plus the file's real type from its first bytes. Nothing is opened or run.

Cases & Reports

PDF and Markdown reports (with a redacted option), a chain-of-custody log, and a copy-ready abuse email for the contacts found.

The hand-off hop: the one address worth trusting

An email collects a Received: header at every server it passes through. NardTrace reads them oldest first. The headers written before the message reached your provider were written by machines the sender controls, so they can claim anything. The hand-off is the first header your own provider wrote — and the IP it recorded is the one NardTrace reports as the sender-side address.

Sender-controlled — forgeable

  1. 1
    ASSUMPTION
    from mail.unknown-host.example (203.0.113.77)
    Written by the sender's own server. The hostname, the IP and the timestamp are all self-reported.
  2. 2
    ASSUMPTION
    from relay7.bulk-sender.example (198.51.100.24)
    Still upstream of your provider. A forged chain usually stops looking tidy right about here.
  3. 3
    CONFIRMED
    by mx1.your-provider.example — from 198.51.100.24
    The hand-off. Your provider observed this connection itself, so the IP in this header is the first one in the chain that nobody upstream could invent.

    This is the address NardTrace runs RDAP against to find the network owner and the abuse desk to report to.

Inside your provider

  1. 4
    CONFIRMED
    by filter.your-provider.example
    Internal routing. Useful for timestamps and delay, not for attribution.
  2. 5
    CONFIRMED
    by imap.your-provider.example — delivered
    Your mailbox. The newest header in the stack.

Addresses and hostnames above are the reserved documentation ranges from RFC 5737 and RFC 2606 — they are examples, not a real sender. What the hand-off gives you is the network responsible for the connection and the abuse contact for it. It is not a person, and it is not a location.

Every finding is labelled by how it is known

Each finding states what was found, where the evidence came from, what it means, what it does not establish, how confident NardTrace is, and what to do next.

CONFIRMED

Seen directly in the evidence: headers, file bytes, link text.

EXTERNAL INTEL

Reported by a third party: registries, DNS, geolocation, reputation services.

ASSUMPTION

An inference, such as which provider sent the mail. Reasonable, but it could be wrong.

UNKNOWN

Could not be determined. The error is shown. Results are never filled in with guesses.

Screens

Captured in offline mode, so registry and DNS panels show UNKNOWN.

Ground rules

  • An IP address identifies a network, never a person or a home address. Location is always labelled approximate.
  • NardTrace never visits suspicious links, opens attachments, or scans anyone's servers.
  • Your evidence stays on your machine. API keys come only from environment variables.
  • It is for investigating messages you received, and for reporting them to people who can act lawfully. It is not for unmasking or confronting individuals.

Run it

Open the web edition and start investigating. Prefer to keep everything on your own machine, or want reputation lookups (AbuseIPDB, VirusTotal)? Run the server edition:

git clone https://github.com/thankcheeses/NardTrace.git
cd NardTrace
docker compose up --build
# then open http://localhost:8000 and click “Load demo case”
